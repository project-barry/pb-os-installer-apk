# What's in a device report?

When you tap **Send Device Report** in PB-OS Installer, the app sends a short
note about your handheld to the Project Barry Discord server. You see the
exact text first, and nothing is sent until you tap **Send**.

## What it contains

- The maker and model, for example "Moorechip Retroid Pocket 6"
- The chip, for example "QCS8550"
- The Android version
- The firmware version, which Android calls the "fingerprint" (nothing to do
  with your finger)
- The name of the memory-card slot, and whether the handheld has a "root" tool
- The version of the app

All of this is the same on every handheld of the same model running the same
firmware. One small exception: if you built your own firmware at home, the
firmware version can include your computer's user name.

## What it never contains

Your name, accounts, serial numbers, contacts, photos, location, Wi-Fi
details, or anything stored on your handheld.

The report service sees your internet address, like any website does. It
keeps only a scrambled version of it, for two days, to stop spam.

## How we use it

- To add handhelds the app doesn't recognise yet
- To fix problems on particular models or firmware versions

Reports are posted in a channel that members of the Project Barry Discord can
read. Each handheld can send one report a day. If you'd like yours removed,
ask us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/euPurKCWc4).
